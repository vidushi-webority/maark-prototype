# Project context

## What this is

Concept demonstrations built by **Webority Technologies** to illustrate the
proposed re-development of two ADG HR web applications to Version 2.0:

- **MAARK 2.0**, Maintenance Allowance Automated Record Keeping
- **CUT-HRV 2.0**, Case Update Tool for Human Rights Violation

## Client and reference

Prepared for the Additional Directorate General of Human Rights, IHQ of MoD
(Army), against RFP A/60463, Upgradation and Re-development of Web
Applications.

## Status

Prototype, demonstration only. Every record shown is fictitious. These are
**not** live, official or classified Government of India or Ministry of Defence
systems. The disclaimers on the landing page and inside each prototype are part
of the deliverable and must stay intact.

## Constraints that come from the RFP

- **Air-gapped.** The finished system runs offline, so the prototypes fetch
  nothing from a network. No CDN, no external font, no npm package.
- **On-premise AI.** The assistant is presented as ETAI-compliant and grounded
  in local policy plus live data, never a hosted model.
- **Role-based access.** User, Admin and Super Admin, with SAML 2.0 IAM login
  and an IACA-token second factor. Both are simulated in the prototype.

## Emblem and national symbols

The emblem is a neutral drawn monogram. The Ashoka Chakra, the tricolour and
the national motto were deliberately removed and must not be reintroduced.
